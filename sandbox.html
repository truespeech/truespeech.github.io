A scratch space for tinkering with truespeech. The lexicon is preloaded with a handful of entries from the operating life of a fictional retailer; the notebook below is yours to play in. Looking to learn the language? Start with the tutorial. Need the full grammar? See the reference.

Pinned to truespeech@v0.3.0 · source on GitHub

The data

NorthStar Goods is a mid-size US retailer with two product tiers (consumer, enterprise) operating across four regions. The corpus is ~200 rows of order data, Jan 2025 through Apr 2026, served from an in-browser DuckDB-WASM instance through an OSI semantic layer.
